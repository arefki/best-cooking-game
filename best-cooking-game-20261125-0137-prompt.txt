Build the best cooking game for make.fun: "Sizzle & Serve — Rush Hour Kitchen", an original, complete, offline browser cooking game.

The game: a fast burger-rush game played with mouse and keyboard in a desktop browser from static files (game/index.html + style.css + game.js, no network, no libraries). Orders land on the order board with a patience timer; raw patties go on the 4-slot grill and cook in ~6 seconds (pull them cooked, trash them burnt); the player stacks each ticket's dish on the plate in order (bun bottom, patty, cheese, lettuce, tomato, bun top) and serves before patience runs out. Tips for fast serves, a combo multiplier for streaks, 3 hearts lost to walked-out orders, a 3-minute shift with escalating order speed and recipe length, title / playing / shift-summary screens, restart, and synthesized WebAudio sounds (no audio files). Deterministic scenes for screenshots via ?shot=start|play|serve|end&seed=1.

Deliverables in this entry: main.ts (Node 22, stdlib only — copies game/ and screenshots/ to the output dir and writes dossier.json with the vendored make.fun library), game/ (the playable game), screenshots/ (4 real renders of the game taken in headless Chrome: title, mid-service with a dish mid-assembly, a plate ready to serve, shift summary), vendor/makefun-game.ts (copied from the kit unchanged), README.md with the competition's three live badges, LICENSE (MIT), .makefun/owner.json.

Judging: an AI judge compares games two at a time by their screenshots and an outline of their source, so the screenshots must show a kitchen and an order board, ingredients on the counter, one dish mid-assembly, a timer, and a plate ready to serve — all four shots are staged to show exactly that.
